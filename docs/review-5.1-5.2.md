Section 5.1 contains fourteen teaching units covering Rosen eighth edition §5.1.1–§5.1.8, printed pages 331–350. It introduces the induction principle, hypothesis versus target, correct starting indices, sums and geometric progressions, inequalities, harmonic bounds, divisibility, subset counting and set identities, greedy scheduling, nearest-neighbor reduction, deficient-board tiling, mistaken proofs, and the least-counterexample justification. It includes original worked proofs and checks, plus an original SVG explaining the central L tile. Exercises on pages 350–354 are not reproduced as an answer bank.

Section 5.2 contains eleven teaching units covering §5.2.1–§5.2.5, printed pages 354–362. It explains strong induction, initial blocks, prime-product existence, the equal-pile game, ordinary and strong proofs for packing with 3- and 7-item boxes, polygon triangulation, well-ordering and its domain, division with existence and uniqueness, tournament cycles, and equivalence of the three principles. An original SVG shows the polygon split and side counts. The interior-diagonal lemma is explicitly stated as a supporting geometric result, with its separate textbook proof referenced and its role explained; that optional geometric proof is not reproduced. Exercises on pages 362–365 are not reproduced as an answer bank.

Editorial review by the authoring assistant checked the cached source exposition and relevant original PDF pages. Particular checks: hypotheses do not assume the target; bases match the domain; summation endpoints give the right term count; inequality multipliers are positive; all objects used by an induction hypothesis fall inside its range; reduced scheduling, nearest-neighbor, and tiling instances retain their assumptions. Prime factorization is stated for n ≥ 2 and proved only for existence. The token game handles empty piles separately. Packing uses three bases and an index k − 2 ≥ 12. Division allows negative dividends, requires a positive divisor, and proves uniqueness separately. Set complements were checked visually because cached text omits some overbars. The source's nearest-neighbor index and circular-sounding postage wording are not repeated. This is an authoring review, not independent human review or machine-checked formal proof.
